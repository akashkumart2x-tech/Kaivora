HEALTHCARE STANDARDS

HIPAA Compliance

Last Updated: May 2026

Healthcare providers handling Protected Health Information (PHI) require the highest standard of data containment and security. Kaivora is engineered to be natively compatible with HIPAA requirements, allowing clinics, hospitals, and digital health startups to deploy custom voice and chat receptionists safely.

1. Business Associate Agreements (BAA)

Under HIPAA rules, any software entity interacting with PHI acts as a "Business Associate." Kaivora signs standard **Business Associate Agreements (BAA)** with all our healthcare customers subscribing to our Enterprise custom tiers, formally codifying our compliance commitments and security covenants.

2. Technical Safeguards & PHI Containment

We implement a multi-layered security framework to protect patient data:

  • Dual AES-256 Encryption: Patient details, triage transcripts, and voice files are encrypted both in transit (using TLS 1.3) and at rest using separate unique master keys.
  • Role-Based Access (RBAC): Access to conversational logs is strictly partitioned using strict multi-factor authentication (MFA) and granular permissions.
  • Cryptographic Audit Trails: Every API call, CRM sync attempt, and database login is logged permanently in tamper-proof audit trails.

3. Secure Patient Intake & Triage

When deploying a Kaivora Voice Receptionist to handle patient booking or intake, the agent is restricted by strict prompt boundaries. Triage transcripts containing sensitive diagnosis keywords are immediately parsed, tokenized to mask PHI, and passed directly into your secure, HIPAA-compliant EHR or patient portal endpoints.

4. Zero Data Retention Policy

To eliminate data exposure paths, Kaivora operates a zero-retention architecture for healthcare pools. Conversational recordings and transcript strings are processed in volatile memory, written securely to your target EHR portal, and instantly wiped from Kaivora server records.
