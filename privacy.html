Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

At Kaivora, your privacy is our absolute priority. This Privacy Policy details how Kaivora Inc. ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal data when you interact with our website, our REST APIs, our Agent SDK, or deploy our autonomous AI Employees.

1. Data We Collect

We restrict data collection to the narrowest possible scope required to deliver our AI services. This includes:

  • Account Data: Secure login details (name, email, company website) stored cryptographically when you create an account.
  • Interaction logs: Temporary text transcript logs and streaming voice files captured during client demos or active sessions.
  • API Payload data: Variable credentials and contact records mapped securely during active Hubspot or Salesforce syncing events.

2. Data Containment & Encryption

All collected metrics and payloads are protected by enterprise-grade cryptographic containment protocols. Inbound voice lines and CRM sync variables use secure TLS 1.3 tunnels in transit and AES-256 standard encryption at rest.

3. Zero-Retention Model Training Guarantee

We enforce a strict zero-retention policy for AI model optimization. Transcripts, audio recordings, and customer database entries are kept completely isolated in secure container structures, deleted automatically within 30 days, and are **never** shared, leased, or utilized to train open-source or commercial third-party LLMs.

4. GDPR & Consumer Rights

In alignment with GDPR regulations, users have complete rights to request data access, rectification, portability, and permanent erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"). To trigger an immediate erasure of all your account metrics, please contact our Data Protection Officer at thekaivora.ai@gmail.com.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use simple functional cookies solely to maintain active dashboard login sessions. We execute zero marketing trackers or behavioral surveillance scripts.
